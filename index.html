    David M. Forsyth

  • December 12, 2025

    Does Salary Inequality Affect Team Success? Evidence from Major League Baseball

    David M. Forsyth

    Across 26 franchises from 1985 to 2016, pay gaps inside a team do not explain winning once payroll and on-field production are held constant.

    Does Telemedicine Save Lives? Evidence on the Effect of Telemedicine Parity Laws on Mortality Rates

    David M. Forsyth · Replication and extension of Chen and Dills (2024)
