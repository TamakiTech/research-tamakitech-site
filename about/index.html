Author

David M. Forsyth

David M. Forsyth writes the papers on this site. He holds a Master of Arts in Economics from the University of Missouri. The papers were prepared in the Department of Economics there.

He is the founder of Tamaki Technologies LLC. The research draws on that economics training and on more than 25 years of work in pension administration, automated benefit calculations, systems analysis, software testing, and data management.

The papers

  • Does Telemedicine Save Lives? Prepared for ECONOM 8413, Research Workshop I, with Dr. Vitor Trindade. December 1, 2025. The paper replicates and extends Chen and Dills, Southern Economic Journal 91(1): 12–37 (2024).
  • Does Salary Inequality Affect Team Success? Prepared for ECONOM 8473, Applied Econometrics, with Dr. Alyssa Carlson. December 12, 2025.

Each paper page states the method and the data sources. The PDF is the full paper. Data files and Stata code are not posted with the papers.
