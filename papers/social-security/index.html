Social Security’s Taxable Wage Maximum

David M. Forsyth
Notes opened November 7, 2026. This is not a finished paper, and there is no PDF yet.

Question

Social Security retirement benefits are financed mainly by a payroll tax on earnings up to an annual maximum. Benefits are also calculated from those covered earnings. Raising or removing that maximum is therefore two different changes, depending on the rule for benefits.

  • Tax the additional earnings and do not count them toward future benefits.
  • Tax the additional earnings and count them toward future benefits.

The first raises revenue only. The second raises revenue and future benefits. The chart below does not estimate either change. It shows the combined trust funds’ income and cost, which is the flow a payroll-tax change would enter.
